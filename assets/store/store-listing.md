## Full description (max 4000 characters)

The water is coming. Can you build the pipe in time?

Leak Out is a pipe-building puzzle game where every second counts. Connect the start to the finish with the pipe sections you're dealt, before the liquid starts flowing and finds a gap to leak out of.

HOW IT WORKS
A countdown starts the moment a level begins. Drag pipe sections from your stack onto the grid and lay a route from the start pipe to the finish. When the countdown hits zero the liquid starts flowing, and it follows your pipes wherever they lead. Reach the finish and you win. Leave an open end and it leaks.

You can't choose your next piece. Your stack shows what's coming, but only the bottom section can be placed, so you have to plan ahead and find a home for awkward pieces. Change your mind? You can swap out a pipe the liquid hasn't reached yet, but rebuilding takes time, and the flow won't wait.

PIECES WITH A TWIST
• Straights and corners: the backbone of every route.
• Cross sections: two channels that never mix. Run the flow through the same cross twice for a big bonus.
• Reservoirs: slow tanks that hold the liquid back and buy you precious seconds, if you can fit a straight run around them.

SCORE, STARS AND MASTERY
Every pipe the liquid passes through earns points. Corners are worth more than straights, and a double pass through a cross pays off most of all. Pipes you placed but never used cost you points, so a tidy pipeline scores better than a sprawling one. Earn up to three stars per level and come back to perfect your routes.

Finished your route early? Hit fast forward and watch the liquid race to the finish.

A GROWING CHALLENGE
24 hand-made levels take you from small, relaxed grids to large boards with walls in the way, faster flow and shorter countdowns. Larger levels let you pan and pinch-zoom across the board. Unlock each level by beating the one before it.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• A clean, glowing look built around flowing liquid and brass fittings
• Satisfying sound effects and haptic feedback with every placement
• A relaxing soundtrack, with separate volume controls for music and effects
• Google Play Games achievements to unlock along the way
