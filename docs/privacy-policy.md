# Leak Out privacy policy

*Effective date: 1 October 2026*

This policy explains what information the Android game **Leak Out** (package `nl.hexmaster.leakout`) handles, where it is kept and what control you have over it. Leak Out is made by HexMaster ("we", "us"), based in the Netherlands.

The short version: Leak Out has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device. The only time data leaves the app is when you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, which then keeps your achievements with Google.

## 1. What the app stores on your device

Leak Out saves the following on your device, so the game remembers where you are:

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings**: sound and music on/off and volume, vibration on/off | To apply your preferences each time you play. |
| **Music rotation**: which background track played last | So the next session starts with a different track. |
| **Level history**: for every level you finished, your best score and the stars earned with it | To show stars and highscores, and to unlock levels. |
| **Achievements** (once achievements are released): which achievements you unlocked and when, progress towards incremental achievements, and game statistics such as the number of pipe sections placed, the number of lost levels and the current losing streak per level | To decide and show achievements, including when you are not signed in to Play Games. |

This data is about how you play the game. It contains no name, email address, contacts, location, photos or other personal details, and it is not linked to an identity unless you sign in to Google Play Games (see section 2).

We do not receive any of this data. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

**Android backup.** Leak Out allows Android's standard app backup. If you have turned on backup on your device, Android may include the data above in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new device. This backup is run by Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## 2. Google Play Games (achievements)

Achievements are planned for an upcoming version of Leak Out. They use **Google Play Games Services**, and signing in is optional: the game, including the achievements screen, works fully without it.

When you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, or tap **Sign in** on the settings screen:

- The app checks your Play Games sign-in status when it starts. This happens without showing any screen.
- The app sends your achievement unlocks and achievement progress to Google Play Games, which stores them with your Play Games profile so they appear in Play Games and carry over to other devices.
- The app receives your Play Games **display name**, which it shows on the settings screen. It does not store it.
- Google Play Games may show your achievements to others according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.

Google processes this data as an independent controller under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Games Services terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms). Through the Google Play Console we can see aggregated statistics, such as the percentage of players who unlocked an achievement, but not your Google account details.

You can sign out of or delete your Play Games profile, and manage its visibility, in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

## 3. What the app does not do

- No account or registration with us.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics, tracking or profiling tools.
- No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or other personal data. The app requests no such permissions.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties, other than the achievement data you choose to send to Google Play Games.
- No servers of our own: we do not collect or store any of your data.

## 4. Google Play

You install Leak Out through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installs, crash reports and app performance data ("Android vitals") as part of operating Google Play and according to your device settings. We may see this information in aggregated or anonymised form in the Play Console and use it only to fix problems and improve the game. This processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Your choices and rights

Because the data stays on your device, you are in control of it:

- **Reset progress** on the settings screen deletes your level history: finished levels, highscores and stars. Achievements and game statistics are kept, because Google Play Games cannot take back an achievement once it has been unlocked.
- **Clearing the app's storage** (Android settings > Apps > Leak Out > Storage > Clear storage) or **uninstalling** the app deletes all of the data in section 1 from your device.
- **Google Play Games data** is managed by Google; see section 2.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide, but you can always contact us with a question or a request. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in the Netherlands this is the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## 6. Children

Leak Out is a puzzle game suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Signing in to Google Play Games is subject to Google's own age requirements and parental controls (such as Google Family Link).

## 7. Security

Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google Play Games is handled by Google's software and is encrypted.

## 8. Changes to this policy

When the app starts handling data differently, for example when achievements are released or a new feature is added, we will update this policy and its effective date. The current version is always available at this page, and its history can be seen in the [repository](https://github.com/hexmasternl/leak-out/commits/main/docs/privacy-policy.md).
