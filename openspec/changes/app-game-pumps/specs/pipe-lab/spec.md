## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Shows every section
The Pipe Lab SHALL show one tile for every pipe section (`H`, `V`, `LT`, `LB`, `RT`, `RB`, `X`, `RES`, `PUMP`), each labelled with its section id, drawn with the pipe tile renderer.

#### Scenario: All eight tiles
- **WHEN** the Pipe Lab opens
- **THEN** it shows nine labelled tiles, one per section, without liquid
